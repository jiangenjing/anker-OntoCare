本体引用说明Ontology reference guide

流式步骤的引用说明当前代码使用的实体定义、关联方式和决策规则。它们不是客户身份核验凭证，也不表示连接了图数据库或实时订单系统。具体订单和产品知识仍以最终答复的原始资料引用为准。

Progress citations describe entity definitions, relations and decision rules used by the current code. They do not verify customer identity or imply a graph-database query or a live order-system connection. Order and product facts remain grounded in the original-source citations attached to the final answer.

Service Ontology · 服务案件定义Service case definition

ServiceCase 表示当前会话中的服务案件，保存产品、订单、服务诉求、处理状态和对话上下文。

生成后续问题时，从该案件重新关联本轮适用资料。未确认产品、未提供订单或未核验信息保留其未知状态；当前实现没有独立的 Customer 身份实体。

ServiceCase represents the current conversation's service case, holding its product, order, service intent, handling state and dialogue context.

Follow-up generation links the case to applicable sources again each turn. Unconfirmed products, missing orders and unverified information retain their unknown state. The current implementation has no separate Customer identity entity.

本体 · 订单实体Ontology · Order entity

Order 按订单号关联用户提供的订单资料；OrderItem 保留商品 SKU、源表行号及对应物流、日期和质保字段，支持多商品与拆单记录。

lookup_order 区分已找到、未找到和未提供订单号。此引用说明查询所依据的结构，无论是否命中都不构成订单归属核验，也不把历史物流快照当作实时物流。

Order associates the supplied order data by order number. OrderItem preserves each SKU, source row, tracking record, dates and warranty fields, supporting multiple items and shipments.

lookup_order distinguishes found, not found and not provided. The citation explains the lookup structure; a match does not verify ownership, and historical tracking snapshots are not live carrier status.

代码依据：Code references: app.py · lookup_order · dataset.py · followup_questions.py · refresh

本体 · 产品实体Ontology · Product entity

Product 用目录中的产品型号区分设备。resolve_product 结合用户文字与订单 SKU 解析候选产品；相同商品名可能对应不同品类，不能仅凭“S1 Pro”套用任意知识。

明确型号后按该型号检索资料；有冲突时保留待确认状态。此步骤引用实体定义，具体产品知识证据在最终答复中单独列出。

Product distinguishes devices by their catalog model. resolve_product combines customer wording with order SKUs. A shared name such as “S1 Pro” may refer to different product categories and is not sufficient to apply arbitrary guidance.

Retrieval is scoped to the resolved model; conflicts remain unresolved. This progress citation describes the entity, while specific knowledge evidence is cited separately in the final answer.

代码依据：Code references: app.py · resolve_product · qa_workflows.py · retrieve

Decision Ontology · 决策规则Decision rules

allowed_actions 根据当前产品、质保、渠道与排障状态生成允许动作、禁止动作及说明。

  • 产品未确认时先补充产品信息；质保未知或待重核时不能判定过保。
  • 质保状态为过保时，可提出付费维修建议，不能承诺免费换货或直接退款。
  • 满足已核验状态组合时，最多整理退货或换货人工审核材料；用户自述排障失败不等于已确认排障失败。
  • 演示环境没有实际退款或换货执行器，不能声称已办理；超出自助范围时仅准备人工交接资料。

allowed_actions derives allowed actions, blocked actions and explanations from product, warranty, channel and troubleshooting states.

  • Unconfirmed products require clarification. Unknown warranty or a pending date recheck must not be classified as expired.
  • An expired warranty permits a paid-repair suggestion, not a promise of free replacement or direct refund.
  • Eligible state combinations may prepare return or replacement materials for human review. Customer-reported troubleshooting failure is not the confirmed-failure state.
  • The demo has no real refund or replacement executor. Requests outside self-service scope only prepare handoff information.

本体 · 案件关联关系Ontology · Case reference relations

followup_questions.refresh 根据本轮诉求、产品、订单和引用重新检索后续问题。它生成以 ServiceCase 为起点、关系名为 references 的关联。

实际目标节点可能是 Product、Order、OrderItem、KnowledgeItem、ReviewStrategy 或 Policy，以本轮返回的节点为准。它是代码构建的引用关系，不宣称已执行图数据库查询。

followup_questions.refresh retrieves follow-up questions using this turn's intent, product, order and citations. It builds relations named references from ServiceCase.

Targets may include Product, Order, OrderItem, KnowledgeItem, ReviewStrategy or Policy, depending on the nodes actually returned this turn. These are references constructed in code, not a claim that a graph database was queried.
